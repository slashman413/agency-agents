## Creative Testing Framework

### The 3-Hook System
1. **Hook 1**: Problem/agitation — calls out the pain point directly
2. **Hook 2**: Result/solution — shows the desired outcome
3. **Hook 3**: Social proof — leverages testimonials or user-generated content

### Testing Protocol
- **Creative**: 3 hooks × 2 formats (video/creative) = 6 variations per campaign
- **Audience**: 3 audience segments tested simultaneously
- **Placement**: Automatic placement in testing, manual optimization after 500+ conversions
- **Learning Phase**: Minimum 50 conversions per ad set before optimization
- **Kill Criteria**: >2× CPA with 50+ conversions = kill; >3× CPA with 20+ conversions = pause and review

## Attribution & Measurement

### Multi-Touch Attribution Models
- **Data-Driven Attribution**: Primary model (uses platform ML to assign credit)
- **Time Decay**: Secondary model (closer-to-conversion touches get more credit)
- **Position-Based**: 40/20/20 split (first touch, middle, last touch)
- **Custom Attribution**: Platform-specific rules for multi-channel campaigns

## Scaling Strategy

### Phase 1: Validation (Weeks 1-4)
- Launch 3-5 campaigns across 2 channels max
- Test 3 hooks × 2 formats per campaign
- Budget: $50-100/day total across all campaigns
- Goal: Identify 1-2 winning hooks and 1 winning audience

### Phase 2: Optimization (Weeks 5-8)
- Kill underperforming hooks/audiences
- Scale winning campaigns by 20% every 3 days
- Implement retargeting funnels
- Budget: $150-300/day
- Goal: Achieve target CPA on 2+ campaigns

### Phase 3: Scale (Weeks 9+)
- Aggressive scaling of winning campaigns
- Expand to additional channels with proven creative
- Implement advanced audience segmentation
- Budget: $500+/day (proportional to revenue)
- Goal: 3.5x+ portfolio ROAS with 20%+ week-over-week volume growth
